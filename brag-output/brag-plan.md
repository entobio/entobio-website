# Brag Plan: EntoBio Consult

## What is this app?
EntoBio Consult is India's premier independent insect taxonomy, biodiversity research, and scientific macro imaging laboratory founded by renowned taxonomist Dr. H. M. Yeshwanth (Ph.D., FRES).

## The angle
"Taxonomy is the layer beneath all of biology. And almost nobody can still do it."
EntoBio Consult doesn't just do insect research—it reveals the unseen world. In a scientific landscape that has largely abandoned pure morphology and specimen curation, Dr. Yeshwanth operates at the microscopic frontier, describing 150 species new to science and having 3 taxa named in his honour. The video treats insect taxonomy not as dusty biology, but as high-precision optical discovery: a darkened laboratory where glowing specimens emerge through razor-sharp focus racks and scientific reticles.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
**The Microscopic Descent:**
Rapid optical zoom-in through four orders of magnitude:
`1 METRE` (the grassland) → `1 CENTIMETRE` (the leaf) → `1 MILLIMETRE` (the insect) → `200 MICROMETRES` (the wing).
*“This is where he works.”*

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Focus Rack & Reticle Reveal:** An ultra-high-resolution macro specimen (`hero-specimen.png`) resolves from blur to razor-sharp magnification under a scientific measurement reticle and glowing amber optical arc, tagged *Hypseloecus sandalum* YESHWANTH, 2014.
2. **The Verified Record (The HUD):** 
   - `150` SPECIES DESCRIBED NEW TO SCIENCE
   - `36` PEER-REVIEWED PUBLICATIONS
   - `16` YEARS IN THE FIELD
3. **The Eponymous Taxa (Scientific Immortality):**
   Three taxa named in his honour by the global entomological community:
   *Evacanthus yeshwanthi* · *Oryctopterus yeshwanthi* · *Axiagastus yeshwanthi*
   *“When a colleague names a species after you, it is the field’s way of recording that you changed what it knows.”*

## Outro / punchline
The manifesto hammer:
*“A species without a name cannot be protected.”*
Resolving to the brand reveal:
*“Everything begins with a name.”*
**ENTOBIO CONSULT** · Bengaluru, India · entobioconsult.com

## User flow worth showing
1. **Descent & Examination:** Calibrating scale from field environment down to 200 micrometres on the specimen.
2. **Taxonomic Determination:** Specimen focus rack, morphological reticle lock, scientific binomial attribution.
3. **The Lifetime Record:** Verified evidence of 150 species described and peer-reviewed worldwide publications.

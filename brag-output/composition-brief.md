## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic` / `polished`
- Creative direction: "Dark laboratory instrument, glowing specimen — high-prestige scientific discovery"
- Interpretation: Ultra-clean, authoritative typography, deep obsidian/void canvas, glowing specimen visuals, and tactile optical audio cues.
- Angle: An uncompromising look at the microscopic frontier of biodiversity research.
- Hook: The rapid 4-step microscopic scale descent down to 200 micrometres in the first 3.7 seconds.
- Outro: The definitive manifesto statement culminating in the EntoBio Consult mark.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler graphics
  - Distracting cartoon transitions

## Storyboard
- Scene 1: **The Scale Descent** (0.00s - 3.70s) — Scale counter 1m → 1cm → 1mm → 200µm with laboratory reticle and location stamp.
- Scene 2: **The Specimen Reveal (Focus Rack)** (3.70s - 8.96s) — Blur-to-sharp focus rack with optical measurement reticle, degree ring, and scientific binomial lock.
- Scene 3: **The Record (150 Species & Honors)** (8.96s - 13.70s) — Split-screen HUD with verified statistics (150 species, 36 papers) and the 3 eponymous taxa cards.
- Scene 4: **The Manifesto & Brand Lockup** (13.70s - 19.00s) — Monolithic manifesto lines resolving into the final EntoBio Consult brand reveal.
